1885. Van Gogh finishes The Potato Eaters — five peasants, one lamp, potatoes on the table. Critics call it dark and clumsy. It doesn't sell. In that world a canvas costs about what a labourer earns in 53 workdays.

Today. That canvas sits in the Van Gogh Museum — priceless, never leaving. But when a major Van Gogh does move, it moves for ~$70M. That's 1,118 years of median full-time pay. The street didn't just fall behind. It changed planets.
