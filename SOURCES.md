# Sources

Primary anchors. Accessed Oct 2026. Full citations in footnotes of `index.html`.

## The painting

- Van Gogh Museum — *The Potato Eaters* (s0005V1962), Nuenen Apr–May 1885, oil on canvas 82×114 cm. Collection-held.
- MoMA / Rijksmuseum / NGA — *The Potato Eaters* lithograph entries (1885) confirming date/medium.
- Christie's — *The Potato Eaters* lithograph lot essay (rare print, 18 known impressions).
- Open Culture / Martin Bailey (The Art Newspaper) — *The Red Vineyard* as the only show-sale in Van Gogh's lifetime (Anna Boch, Brussels 1890, 400 francs).

## Wages & prices, 1885

- University of Missouri Library Guide "Prices and Wages by Decade: 1880–1889" — Michigan 1885 day rates (miners $2–2.25, farm hands $1.50–1.75, carpenters $3, masons $4).
- NBER ch. "Course of Money Wages 1860–1890" (Aldrich + Bulletin 18) — building trades ~$2.55–2.94/day; manufacturing avg ~$1.44–1.61/day in 1885.
- US Dept of State, *Money and Prices in Foreign Countries*, vol. 8 (1885) — Netherlands wages p. 54 (~ƒ1/day); France/Lyons wages 1870–1896; Italy 1886.
- 1895 US Consular report — European city wages 1870–1896 (France/Italy/Germany/Switzerland/Russia/England/Belgium).

## Prices, now (Van Gogh market)

- Guinness World Records — Most expensive Van Gogh at auction: *Orchard with Cypresses* $117,180,000, Christie's NY, 9 Nov 2022.
- HENI / Christie's — *Verger avec cyprès* (1888) $117.2M (Paul Allen sale); previous record *Laboureur dans un champ* $81.31M (13 Nov 2017); *Portrait du Dr Gachet* $75M (1990, $82.5M with premium).
- NYT (May 2023) — *Vase with Daisies and Poppies* (1914 sale $61.8M) offered privately at ~$70M.
- Rarest.org / Masterworks / vggallery auctions page — cross-checks: Self-Portrait Without Beard $71.5M (1998), Wheatfield with Cypresses $57M (1993), Peasant Woman $47.5M (1997).

## Wages & prices, now

- BLS TED (Feb 2026) + News Release USDL-26-0119 — Median full-time weekly earnings $1,204 in 2025 (121.5M workers; women $1,089 / men $1,326).
- BLS OEWS May 2025 (USDL-26-0725, released May 2026) — Mean annual wage $69,770; median $49,500 baseline.
- Wage.is (2026-03/05) — France avg €3,500/mo, Netherlands avg €3,900/mo (context only; US is the charted anchor).
- Bread (~$3/loaf) and US median home (~$412k, 2025) are labelled estimates — see `data/figures.json` notes.
